# Retrospective

Review what was expected, what happened, what helped, what failed, which assumptions were wrong, and what should change.

Link lessons to evidence such as commits, issues, CI results, incidents, or user feedback. Promote only durable lessons into project/global documents. Every proposed process change must identify the problem it fixes and avoid ceremony without measurable benefit.
