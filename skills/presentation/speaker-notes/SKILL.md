# Speaker Notes

Speaker notes support delivery; they do not replace the slide.

## Include when useful
- the key point to say
- supporting evidence or example
- transition to the next slide
- emphasis or pause cue
- timing cue for long talks
- likely question or objection

## Rules
Keep notes scannable. Do not duplicate the slide word-for-word unless a verbatim script is explicitly requested. For self-read/forwarded decks, move essential context onto slides rather than hiding it in notes.
