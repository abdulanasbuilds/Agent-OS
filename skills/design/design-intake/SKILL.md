# Design Intake

Do not guess the visual direction from a vague request.

## Ask only what changes the design

1. What is the product/service and what business problem should the page solve?
2. Who is the primary audience and what should they feel or do?
3. What is the primary action or conversion goal?
4. What brand assets, colors, logos, photography, illustrations, or existing design systems must be preserved?
5. Are there reference sites/screenshots the user likes or explicitly dislikes? Ask what specifically they like/dislike.
6. What platform, framework, viewport targets, content volume, and performance constraints apply?
7. Is the surface informational, transactional, editorial, dashboard-like, experimental, or a mixture?
8. What level of motion/interaction is appropriate, and are there accessibility constraints?

## Output

Produce a compact Design Brief before implementation. Record known facts, assumptions, open decisions, and the proposed direction. If the user has not chosen a direction and the page is high visibility, route to `/design-variants`.

## Anti-pattern
Never ask 20 questions when 5 will resolve the major design decisions. Do not interrogate for information that has no impact on the design.
