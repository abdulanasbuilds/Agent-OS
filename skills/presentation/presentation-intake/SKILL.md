# Presentation Intake

Do not begin styling until the communication constraints are understood.

## Ask only decision-changing questions
- What is the audience?
- What should they understand, believe, decide, or do?
- Is this live, recorded, forwarded, self-read, or printed?
- How long will it be delivered and roughly how many slides are appropriate?
- What source material is authoritative?
- What output is needed: editable PPTX, PDF, web/HTML, or another format?
- Is there an existing brand/design system?
- Are there reference decks or visual examples?

## Business context
For commercial decks identify buyer, offer, proof, objection, desired action, and business consequence. For internal or educational decks identify the decision or learning outcome.

## Output
Produce a compact presentation brief before narrative/design work. Mark unknowns rather than inventing answers.
