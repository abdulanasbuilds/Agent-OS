# /website

Load `design-router` first with `website` context.

Prioritize business objective, audience, market/category, trust signals, content hierarchy, brand direction, real visual references, photography/illustration needs, typography, responsive layout, interaction, motion, accessibility, SEO-visible structure, and performance.

For high-visibility pages where the visual direction is genuinely open, use the design-variants workflow before committing.

Do not default to generic AI/SaaS patterns or invent brand facts, testimonials, people, facilities, products, or assets.
