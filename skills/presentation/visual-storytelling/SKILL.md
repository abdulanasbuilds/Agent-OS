# Visual Storytelling

Show the important idea whenever a visual can communicate it more directly than prose.

## Choose visuals by message
- Comparison → side-by-side or structured contrast.
- Process → flow or sequence.
- Change over time → timeline or trend.
- Geography → map when location matters.
- System → architecture or relationship diagram.
- Magnitude → appropriately scaled chart or big number.
- Product behavior → real screenshot or controlled demonstration.
- Concept → concrete diagram or restrained metaphor.

## Rules
Use real evidence where available. Do not fabricate screenshots, people, customers, metrics, or product states. Label illustrative visuals when they are not factual evidence.

## Reference use
Search the presentation reference catalog for relevant visual precedents, then recreate principles instead of copying protected design work.
