# Design Direction

Commit to one coherent visual point of view before styling components.

## Consider
- brand character and cultural context
- audience expectations and trust requirements
- content density and hierarchy
- product category conventions worth keeping or breaking
- light/dark environment
- typography personality
- shape language, borders, surfaces, texture, and imagery
- motion intensity

## Anti-slop test
Reject a direction if it could describe dozens of unrelated AI-generated SaaS sites with the same typography, gradient, card grid, and hero treatment.

## Distinctiveness
Prefer one or two memorable design signatures over ten decorative effects. A signature may be typography, composition, material treatment, imagery, interaction rhythm, or editorial structure.

## Output
State the chosen direction, why it fits the product, what is intentionally excluded, and the design signatures that should remain consistent across pages.
