# Wayfinder

Use when the work is too large or uncertain for one focused session.

Define the destination as a user/product promise and its evidence. Identify only unknowns that block the route. Resolve them in dependency order and keep decisions at the promise level rather than prescribing files, function signatures, pixels, or implementation constants.

Bound every stored map artifact. Never append indefinitely to size-limited tracker fields. When the route is clear, hand the resolved decisions to the normal spec/implementation flow.

Wayfinding is planning, not a license to build, deploy, or mutate production.
