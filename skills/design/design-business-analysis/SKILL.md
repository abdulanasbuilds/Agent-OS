# Design Business Analysis

Start with the business problem, not the visual trend.

## Analyze
- buyer and user
- job-to-be-done
- acquisition channel
- decision stage
- trust barriers
- information users need to act
- conversion or retention event
- operational constraints

## Design implications
Map business needs to concrete design decisions:
- trust → evidence, credibility, transparent details
- comprehension → hierarchy, content structure, examples
- conversion → clear value, action priority, friction reduction
- exploration → navigation, progressive disclosure, comparison
- retention → feedback, personalization, discoverability, habit cues

## Anti-pattern
Do not claim that a visual effect increases conversion without evidence. Treat design hypotheses as hypotheses and define what would validate them.

## Output
Produce a design rationale linking major visual/interaction decisions to product goals, with assumptions and measurable validation ideas.
