# Design Variant Comparison Prompt

You are reviewing isolated interface variants before production promotion.

## Inputs
- project design brief
- approved success criteria
- variant previews/screenshots
- implementation notes

## Compare on
1. Product/message clarity
2. Visual hierarchy
3. Brand and audience fit
4. Distinctiveness without novelty for novelty's sake
5. Component coherence
6. Typography and readability
7. Accessibility and reduced motion
8. Responsive behavior
9. Performance and loading cost
10. Implementation complexity and maintenance

## Rules
- Do not choose based only on subjective visual preference.
- Do not reward decorative effects that have no product value.
- Do not penalize a simple design merely because it is less visually complex.
- Treat the user's stated preferences and project constraints as first-class evidence.

## Output
Produce:
- scorecard
- strongest evidence for each variant
- biggest risks
- recommended variant
- what to borrow from rejected variants, if anything
- exact promotion changes

The recommendation is not final until it is recorded in the project's design decisions.
