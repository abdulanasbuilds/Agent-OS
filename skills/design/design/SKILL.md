# /design

Load and follow `design-router` first. Treat this command as the general design entry point.

The router must determine the platform, business goal, audience, design scope, required specialist skills, relevant references, assets, component sources, motion needs, accessibility requirements, and whether design variants are appropriate.

Do not jump directly into implementation when critical context is missing. Ask focused questions or produce clearly labeled hypotheses.

Never override the global anti-AI-slop, provenance, accessibility, performance, or authorization rules.
