# Presentation Typography

Typography must survive projection, screen sharing, mobile review, and export.

## Process
1. Define title, headline, body, label, data, caption, and notes roles.
2. Choose families for brand fit, legibility, language coverage, available weights, and performance.
3. Establish scale, line height, tracking, measure, and wrapping rules.
4. Test at presentation distance and in grayscale/low-quality screen sharing.
5. Ensure numerals remain distinguishable for metrics and tables.

## Anti-trend rule
Never choose a font just because it is fashionable in AI/SaaS design. Record the reason for the chosen family and verify its license.
