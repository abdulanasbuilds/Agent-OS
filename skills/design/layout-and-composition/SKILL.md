# Layout and Composition

Solve hierarchy before decoration.

## Process
1. Identify the page's job and information hierarchy.
2. Establish container width, reading measure, column structure, alignment anchors, and vertical rhythm.
3. Choose composition patterns based on content rather than defaulting to cards or bento grids.
4. Define responsive transformations for small, medium, and large screens.
5. Preserve important relationships when content expands, wraps, or disappears.

## Composition toolkit
Use grids, asymmetry, editorial rhythm, whitespace, overlap, scale, and controlled density deliberately. A strong composition may be quiet; it does not need effects to feel designed.

## Anti-slop rule
Do not force every page into a hero + three cards + bento + CTA formula. The layout should emerge from the product's content and task model.

## Verification
Test at representative mobile, tablet, and desktop widths. Check overflow, wrapping, visual hierarchy, content extremes, and interaction reachability.
