# Grilling

Ask only high-leverage questions that cannot be answered from the repository, approved sources, tests, or existing artifacts.

Challenge ambiguous terms, hidden assumptions, edge cases, irreversible choices, and conflicts with existing project decisions. Prefer one question at a time. Update durable project context when a term or decision is settled.

Stop when the next workflow can proceed safely. Do not invent an answer simply to close the interview and do not treat a resolved product decision as permission for unrelated actions.
