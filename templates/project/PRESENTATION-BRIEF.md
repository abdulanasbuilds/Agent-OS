# Presentation Brief

## Purpose
What should the audience understand, believe, decide, or do?

## Audience
Who is attending or reading? What do they already know?

## Context
Live / recorded / forwarded / self-read / printed.

## Duration and size
Expected speaking time and approximate slide count.

## Business or decision context
Buyer/user, decision owner, stakes, objections, proof required, desired action.

## Content sources
Authoritative documents, data, references, existing materials.

## Narrative
Core message(s), arc, major sections, slide sequence.

## Visual direction
Brand, type, color, imagery, diagram/chart language, tone.

## Output
PPTX / PDF / HTML / other.

## Accessibility
Target audience needs, captions, alt text, reading order, contrast, reduced motion if relevant.

## Constraints
Brand rules, platform restrictions, deadlines, editability, file-size constraints, privacy or licensing constraints.
