# Slide Composition

A slide is a viewport with a communication hierarchy.

## Rules
- Establish one dominant focal point.
- Use a clear reading path and alignment system.
- Choose slide type from the message: statement, comparison, process, data, framework, image-led, quote, code, timeline, map, diagram, appendix, or another justified form.
- Avoid repeated identical layouts when the narrative needs visual rhythm.
- Keep text legible at presentation distance.
- Split dense content instead of shrinking type until it becomes unreadable.
- Use whitespace deliberately; do not fill empty space merely because it exists.

## Verification
Review the 3-second scan, arm's-length readability, alignment, overflow, hierarchy, contrast, and relation to neighboring slides.
