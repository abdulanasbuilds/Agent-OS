# Data Storytelling

Start with the question the audience must answer from the data.

## Workflow
1. Identify the analytical task: compare, trend, rank, distribution, relationship, composition, or uncertainty.
2. Verify source, units, timeframe, denominator, and calculation.
3. Choose the simplest chart or visual form that answers the question.
4. Highlight the relevant signal without distorting the data.
5. Add concise source/context notes where needed.
6. Check labels, axes, legends, color usage, and accessibility.

## Rules
Do not manipulate axes, omit material context, imply causality without evidence, or invent values. Avoid chartjunk and dense legends.
